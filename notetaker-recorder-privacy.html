Privacy policy: Notetaker Recorder JLN

Last updated: 2026-11-14.

Notetaker Recorder JLN is a Windows app used only by its owner, to record the owner's classes and meetings for the owner's own notes server.

What the app records

The app records only when the user starts a recording, either from its tray menu or by answering a reminder. It records the sound of one chosen application (for example a video meeting) or the microphone, and takes still pictures of that application's window during the recording. It never records in the background without the user starting it.

Where the data goes

Recordings are saved on the user's computer and then copied to a server that the user runs and controls (the user sets its address in the app's settings). The app also reads a class schedule from that same server. The app sends nothing to the developer, to Microsoft or to any other party.

The app contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It has no account and no sign in.

Keeping and deleting data

Local copies are deleted automatically some days after the server confirms it received them (the number of days is a setting). Uninstalling the app deletes its local settings, logs and recordings. Data on the user's own server is kept and deleted by the user.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on the developer's GitHub page, github.com/ThisIsJLN.
